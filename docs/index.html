Our Premium Menu

Discover our exquisite selection of culinary offerings, crafted with the finest ingredients and presented with elegance.

Galbi

MEAT

Grilled beef short ribs marinated in a sweet and savory sauce typically made with soy sauce, garlic, and sugar. Known for its rich flavor and tender texture.

Bulgogi

MEAT

Thinly sliced, marinated beef grilled or pan-fried. The marinade is a blend of soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and garlic, giving it a mildly sweet and smoky flavor.

Korean Style Chicken

MEAT

Deep-fried chicken pieces coated in a variety of sauces. Popular styles include soy garlic, spicy gochujang-based, or plain crispy. Often served as a snack or main dish.

Cocktail Shrimp

SEAFOOD

Chilled, peeled, and deveined shrimp typically served on a platter with a spicy cocktail sauce (ketchup, horseradish, lemon juice). A classic appetizer.

Shrimp with Garlic Butter

SEAFOOD

Shrimp sautéed in melted butter infused with minced garlic and herbs. A rich, aromatic, and savory preparation.

Teriyaki Salmon

SEAFOOD

Salmon fillet glazed with a sweet and savory Teriyaki sauce (soy sauce, mirin, sugar). The fish is usually grilled or baked, resulting in a slightly caramelized exterior.

Smoked Salmon with Cream Cheese

SEAFOOD

Thin slices of cured and smoked salmon served with a spread of soft cream cheese, often on a bagel or cracker, and sometimes garnished with capers or dill.

Buttered Cod

SEAFOOD

Cod fish fillet baked or pan-fried, basted with melted butter. A simple preparation that highlights the flaky texture and mild flavor of the cod.

Honey Glazed Cod

SEAFOOD

Cod fish fillet coated with a sticky and sweet glaze made primarily from honey. The glaze adds a delicate sweetness to the mild-flavored fish.

Stir Fried Mushrooms

VEGETABLE

A medley of various mushrooms (like Shiitake, Oyster, and Enoki) quickly sautéed with seasonings, often soy sauce and garlic, for an earthy and umami-rich side dish.

Spicy Green Bean

VEGETABLE

Fresh green beans quickly sautéed or blanched and then tossed in a spicy sauce or seasoning, often containing chili flakes or Sriracha.

Stir-fry Bockchoy

VEGETABLE

Bok choy (Chinese cabbage) quickly sautéed in a wok or pan with garlic and often a light sauce made from soy sauce or oyster sauce. It retains a crisp texture.

California Roll

RICE & NOODLES

A popular inside-out sushi roll typically containing crab meat (often imitation crab/surimi), cucumber, and avocado, wrapped in nori (seaweed) and rice.

Steamed Rice

RICE & NOODLES

Plain, cooked white rice (usually short-grain), prepared by steaming until soft and fluffy. A staple side dish for many meals.

Fried Rice (Chicken or Shrimp)

RICE & NOODLES

Cooked rice stir-fried in a wok with egg, chopped vegetables (e.g., peas, carrots, onions), soy sauce, and your choice of diced chicken or small shrimp.

Lomain (Chicken or Shrimp)

RICE & NOODLES

Chinese egg noodles tossed and stir-fried with vegetables (e.g., cabbage, carrots) and a savory sauce, featuring either sliced chicken or shrimp as the main protein.

Buckwheat Cold Noodles with Vegetable

RICE & NOODLES

Chilled buckwheat noodles (Naengmyeon) served in a tangy, chilled broth, topped with sliced pickled radish, cucumber, and sometimes a hard-boiled egg.

Steamed or Fried Mandu

DUMPLINGS & ROLLS

Korean dumplings that are either steamed for a soft, chewy texture or pan-fried (potstickers/Gun Mandu) for a crispy exterior and tender filling.

Vegetable Eggrolls

DUMPLINGS & ROLLS

Crispy, deep-fried rolls with a thin wheat flour wrapper, typically filled with a shredded mix of cabbage, carrots, and other vegetables.

Garden Salad

SALAD

A simple mixed green salad made from lettuce, tomatoes, cucumbers, and other raw vegetables, often served with a choice of dressing.

Chicken Salad

SALAD

A creamy salad made from shredded or diced cooked chicken, mayonnaise, and various seasonings, often including celery and onions.

Tuna Salad

SALAD

A creamy salad made from flaked canned tuna, mayonnaise, and various seasonings, often including celery and relish.

Egg Salad

SALAD

A creamy salad made from chopped hard-boiled eggs, mayonnaise, mustard, and seasonings.

Fruits & Cheese

FRUITS & CHEESE

An assortment of fresh, seasonal fruits (e.g., grapes, melon, berries) paired with a selection of cheeses (e.g., cheddar, brie, provolone).
